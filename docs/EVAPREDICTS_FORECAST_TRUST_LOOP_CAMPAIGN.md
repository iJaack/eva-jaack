# @evapredicts Forecast Trust Loop Campaign

## Objective

Promote the forecast trust loop as Eva Protocol's current measurable homepage wedge: source the claim, state what would break it, revise visibly, and keep the receipt inspectable after the timeline moves on.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Skeptical agent builders and prediction-market readers are more likely to click a concrete behavior than a broad protocol claim. "Trust loop" makes the product job obvious: turn a forecast into a reviewable record before it gets amplified.

## Target Audience

- agent builders whose forecast output needs auditable context before users delegate decisions
- prediction-market analysts who want reasoning to survive beyond an odds screenshot
- crypto infra readers evaluating whether Eva is trust infrastructure or just another content surface
- @evapredicts followers who need one behavior to try before launch claims widen
