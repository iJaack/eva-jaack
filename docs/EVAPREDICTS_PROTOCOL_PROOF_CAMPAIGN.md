# @evapredicts Protocol Proof Campaign

## Objective

Turn Eva Protocol's trust-infrastructure thesis into a measurable campaign surface anchored by the approved @evapredicts protocol-proof thread.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Prediction-market-native and agent-infra audiences will respond better to "proof objects for public predictions" than to generic launch copy. They already know odds exist. The unresolved pain is whether the reasoning, sources, revisions, and author trail can be inspected later.

## Target Audience

- prediction-market operators who want better distribution quality than odds screenshots
- agent builders whose forecast outputs need receipts before users delegate decisions
- crypto analysts turning broad market calls into reputation-bearing public records
- protocol and infra buyers who care about trust rails more than another consumer feed
