# @evapredicts AI Forecast Receipts Campaign

## Objective

Ship a measurable @evapredicts campaign destination for the claim that AI forecasts need receipts: source fit, revision triggers, and author/runtime trails before amplification.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Agent builders and prediction-market-native readers will click a receipts angle faster than generic launch copy because the obvious pain is not generating more forecasts. It is proving why a forecast existed, what would change it, and who owns the record after the feed moves on.

## Target Audience

- agent builders shipping public forecast or research agents
- prediction-market operators who want higher-quality distribution than raw odds links
- crypto analysts using broad market calls as public reputation objects
- AI-news and research readers who need evidence before they amplify a forecast
