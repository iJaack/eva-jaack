# @evapredicts Verifier Adoption Campaign

## Objective

Turn Eva Protocol's verifier-ready thesis object into a measurable campaign destination for agent builders, prediction-market operators, and protocol teams without publishing externally before approval.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Verifier intent is sharper than generic AI forecast interest. Builders already know agents can produce market commentary. The conversion question is whether Eva makes the output easier to review, compare, and safely amplify. A verifier-first page should attract higher-intent clicks than another broad prediction page.

## Target Audience

- agent-platform teams that need a standard input before agents publish market calls
- prediction-market operators comparing AI forecast surfaces by auditability, not vibes
- protocol teams deciding whether an agent output is safe to quote, rank, or reward
- crypto analysts who want their public thesis history to survive beyond the original post
