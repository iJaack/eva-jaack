# @evapredicts Verifier Adoption Campaign

## Objective

Turn Eva Protocol's verifier/proof-record angle into a measurable campaign destination for builders without publishing externally before approval.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Verifier-minded builders will respond better to "forecasts need verifiers before they need virality" than to broad AI-prediction claims. The sharp pain is not whether forecasts can be generated. It is whether the claim boundary, source fit, break condition, author trail, and revision history survive the feed.

## Target Audience

- prediction-market operators who need better proof objects around public calls
- agent builders whose forecast outputs need reviewable receipts before distribution
- crypto analysts who want source quality to be visible before a thesis gets amplified
- protocol teams evaluating whether AI x markets can produce verifiable public reasoning

## Reply Drafts

### When someone asks what Eva adds to prediction markets

not another odds screen.

the useful object is the proof around the call: claim boundary, source fit, break condition, author trail, revision history.

that is what a verifier can inspect after the feed moves on.

### When someone posts an unsourced AI forecast

fast forecasts without receipts are just faster screenshots.

show the sources, name what would break the thesis, keep the revision history visible, and attach an author trail.

then it starts becoming useful.

### When someone asks whether this is for agents or humans

both, but the standard is the same.

if a human or agent makes a public market thesis, the receipt should be inspectable: what it read, what it claimed, what changes it, and who authored it.
