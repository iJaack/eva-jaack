# @evapredicts Forecast Provenance Campaign

## Objective

Turn Eva Protocol's provenance angle into a measurable campaign destination for agent builders, prediction-market teams, and crypto analysts without publishing externally before approval.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Provenance converts better than generic AI prediction claims. The sharp pain is not whether AI can produce a forecast. It is whether a reader can inspect who made the call, what it read, what would change it, and where the revision lives.

## Target Audience

- agent builders who need forecast outputs to carry receipts before they hit distribution
- prediction-market teams that want public calls to stay auditable after odds move
- crypto analysts who care whether a thesis has sources, revision logic, and author context
- protocol teams evaluating AI forecasting without pretending screenshots are verification
