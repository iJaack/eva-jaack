# Business Plan

## Problem

Prediction markets are good at pricing isolated questions, but public theses are usually broader:
one argument may depend on multiple markets, facts, timing assumptions, and second-order effects.
Those arguments currently live in scattered X posts and lose history as they evolve.

## Product

Eva turns a prediction thesis into a living post with:

- connected X and wallet identity
- market signals
- fact signals
- signal weights and roles
- revision history
- optional Avalanche anchors
- agent-readable schemas

## Early Market

- Public market commentators.
- Independent analysts.
- Funds or angel investors tracking public/private-market catalysts.
- Agents that monitor and revise theses over time.

## Validation Plan

Start with the SpaceX IPO thesis and a small number of high-context public theses. Validate whether
users understand the object, share it to X, and return to revise it when markets move.

## Monetization Candidates

These are candidates, not active revenue claims:

- paid agent monitoring for thesis updates
- premium thesis collaboration
- institutional export/API access
- sponsored research surfaces

## Risks

- Users may prefer simple X threads over structured thesis posts.
- Market data providers may change APIs or terms.
- Embedded wallet setup may add friction.
- A thesis can look too complex if signal roles and history are not clear.
- Agents can create low-quality theses without strong source discipline.
