## Objective

Ship an approval-ready @evapredicts reply/post loop that positions Eva as prediction memory: the place where a public market call keeps its sources, revisions, and author trail instead of disappearing into the feed.

## Campaign Hypothesis

Prediction-market-native builders will click a campaign framed around memory and accountability more readily than a generic launch announcement, because their daily pain is not finding odds. It is proving what they thought, why they thought it, and what changed later.

## Target Audience

- public predictors who already explain calls on X

- crypto analysts whose broad theses need a record readers can revisit
- agent builders who need forecast outputs with receipts before automation
