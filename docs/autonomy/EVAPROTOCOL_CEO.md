# Eva Protocol CEO Autonomy

## Operating Loop

1. Find a broad thesis that cannot be captured by one market.
2. Publish it through Eva with X plus wallet identity.
3. Share the Eva thesis page on X.
4. Watch market odds, closed predictions, and relevant facts.
5. Revise the thesis when the signal picture changes.
6. Preserve the history so readers can see how the argument evolved.

## Canonical Example

The SpaceX IPO liquidity rotation thesis is the first product test. Agents and operators should use
it to evaluate whether Eva can combine multiple prediction markets, facts, and second/third-order
effects into one readable, evolving post.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim trade execution.
- Do not claim article verification or curator staking.
- Do not claim active traction without measured data.
- Confirm the deployer wallet before any deployment or anchor transaction.
- Keep docs, product copy, and `protocol.config.json` aligned.

## Agent Instructions

- Prefer MCP/API reads over page scraping.
- Keep source URLs attached to fact signals.
- Use clear signal roles: core, lateral, second_order, third_order, hedge, contradiction.
- Write revision notes that explain why the thesis changed.
- Ask for approval before posting publicly or signing transactions.
